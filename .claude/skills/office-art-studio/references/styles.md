## Writing a new style

Answer each line with concrete nouns and colours, then join them into one paragraph starting with `STYLE:`.

1. Floor: material + texture + colour (e.g. "pale oak planks with visible grain").
2. Corridors: a contrasting but related floor.
3. Walls: colour + trim + skirting.
4. Desks, chairs, monitors: materials and colours.
5. Soft furnishings: sofas, rugs, cushions.
6. Plants and decor: kind and pots.
7. Light and mood: source and three adjectives.
8. Department accent floors: exactly four named hues.

Keep brand colours to accents (chairs, rugs, trims). Floors in brand colours make the whole office one flat colour.
